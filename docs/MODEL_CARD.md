## Intended use
Ranking and flagging card transactions for **human review** in a demo / research setting.
It is not validated for automated blocking, credit decisions, or any regulated production use.

## Model
XGBoost gradient-boosted trees on V1-V28 (anonymised PCA components), log-amount (standardised, fitted on train only)
and hour-of-day (sin/cos). Imbalance handled by **scale_pos_weight**, chosen by CV PR-AUC on the training split only.

## Limitations and known failure modes
- **Test frauds are few** (75); see the confidence intervals above - they are wide.
- Time-ordered split: performance on later data reflects drift, but only over the span of this dataset (about 2 days for the Kaggle set).
- Fraud patterns change; the model will degrade without retraining. `/monitoring/drift` reports PSI on live inputs but cannot detect label drift.
- Features are anonymised PCA components, so explanations (reason codes) describe the model, not a real-world cause.
- Threshold depends on the configured costs; change `config/train.yaml` costs and re-train if your costs differ.
- Single-process in-memory monitoring and rate limiting; not suitable for multi-replica deployment as-is.
- No fairness / bias assessment is possible: the dataset has no demographic attributes.
